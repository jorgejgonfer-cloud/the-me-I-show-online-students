Plan & Create your Infographic

Plan your infographic

Duración:
30 min
Agrupamiento:
Parejas

A great infographic doesn't happen by accident. Follow these steps to plan yours.

Step 1 — Choose your angle (5 min)

Choose ONE of these focuses:

  • "5 tips for a healthier social media diet"
  • "How to stop doomscrolling tonight"
  • "The phone-free morning: a 7-day plan"
  • "How to unfollow without guilt"
  • Your own idea — discuss it with your teacher first.

Step 2 — Write your 5 tips (15 min)

Write 5 short, clear tips. Each tip should:

  • Use one of the structures from Language for Advice.
  • Be no longer than 12 words.
  • Include a verb of action.

Step 3 — Decide on visuals (5 min)

For each tip, think of: an icon and a colour (pick 2–3 colours maximum).

Step 4 — Add an interactive element

Your infographic must have AT LEAST ONE interactive element: clickable button, hover effect, animation, or embedded video/audio.

Digital tool: Canva or Genially

You will create your infographic using Canva (free version) or Genially.

Quick start with Canva

  1. Go to www.canva.com and log in (or create a free account with your personal email).
  2. Search for "infographic" in the templates.
  3. Pick a template you like — you can change colours, text and icons.
  4. Replace the placeholder text with YOUR 5 tips.
  5. Add at least one interactive element.
  6. When you finish, click "Share" → "Share link".
  7. Copy the link.

Alternative tool: Genially (www.genial.ly) is also free and offers more interactive options.

How to submit your infographic

Duración:
Antes de Sesión 3
Agrupamiento:
Parejas

Where to submit

Microsoft Teams → channel "The Me I Show Online" → Assignments → "Session 2: Infographic".

What to submit

  • The PUBLIC link to your Canva or Genially design.
  • A short message (3–4 sentences) explaining your angle.

Deadline

The day before Session 3 begins, at 23:59.

How it will be assessed

With the rubric "Rúbrica para evaluar una infografía" — see the "Assessment Rubrics" page.
