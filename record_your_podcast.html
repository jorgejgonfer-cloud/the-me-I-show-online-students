Record Your Podcast

Recording guide

Duración:
30 min de aula + casa
Agrupamiento:
Individual o parejas

Option A — Record both voices yourself (recommended)

Record yourself reading both parts. Change your tone, your speed and your distance from the microphone for each character.

Option B — Record with a partner

If you have a classmate, friend or family member willing to play "the phone", record together.

How to record

  1. Use Vocaroo (vocaroo.com) — free, no installation, in your browser.
  2. Or use the Voice Memos app on your phone.
  3. Or use Audacity on your computer (free, open-source).

Find a quiet room. Get close to the microphone (15–20 cm). Don't shout — speak at a normal volume.

Editing (optional but recommended)

  • Cut long silences.
  • Add a short intro music (Bensound or YouTube Audio Library — both free).
  • Add a tiny sound effect (a phone notification at the start).

Whatever tool you use, export/save your final file as .mp3.

How to submit your podcast

Duración:
1 semana después de Sesión 3
Agrupamiento:
Individual

Where to submit

Microsoft Teams → channel "The Me I Show Online" → Assignments → "Session 3: Podcast".

What to submit

  • Your audio file (.mp3 preferred).
  • A short transcript of your podcast (.docx or .pdf) — about 250–300 words.
  • In the comment box: a sentence describing your phone's personality.

Deadline

One week after Session 3, at 23:59.

How it will be assessed

With the rubrics on the "Assessment Rubrics" page: Rúbrica para evaluar una exposición oral + Podcast Rubric.
