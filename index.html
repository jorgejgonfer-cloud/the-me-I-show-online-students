The Me I Show Online

Welcome

Welcome! In this learning unit you are going to explore the way you live online — what you scroll through, what you share, and what your phone really knows about you.

Over three sessions you will read, listen, talk and create. By the end, you will have produced two pieces of work that you can be proud of:

  • An interactive infographic with practical advice for healthier digital habits.
  • A short podcast in English in which you have a conversation with… your own phone.

Both products will be shared with the rest of the class through Microsoft Teams, so you will get to see and hear what your classmates have created too.

Take your time, enjoy the topic, and don't be afraid to be honest about your own digital life. That's where the best ideas come from. 📱

What You Will Learn

By the end of this learning unit, you will be able to:

  • Talk and write about your digital habits using vocabulary related to mobile phones, social media and the Internet.
  • Understand authentic spoken and written texts about technology and well-being at a B1.2 level.
  • Give advice and recommendations using a range of structures (you should…, why don't you…, it's a good idea to…, try …-ing).
  • Plan and design a clear, attractive infographic in English.
  • Plan, script and record a short podcast in English with appropriate intonation and natural turn-taking.
  • Reflect critically on your own relationship with technology.
