SESSION 3: Podcast — A Dialogue With My Phone

Welcome to the Podcast Session!

In our final session, you are going to give your phone a voice — and then have a conversation with it in English.

  • First, we will listen to a model podcast in which someone has a small argument with their phone.
  • Then we will learn useful phrases for natural-sounding dialogue.
  • You will write your own script for a 2–3 minute podcast.
  • And finally, you will record and submit your podcast.

By the end of today, you will have a real audio piece in English that you can share. 🎧
