A Model Infographic

Analyse a model

Duración:
15 min
Agrupamiento:
Parejas


Look at the model infographic above and discuss with a partner:

  1. How many sections does it have? How are they organised?
  2. Where does your eye go first? Why?
  3. What kind of language does it use — long sentences or short bullet points?
  4. How does the colour and the icons help you understand the message?
  5. Can you find any imperatives ("Don't…", "Try to…")? Any modal verbs?
