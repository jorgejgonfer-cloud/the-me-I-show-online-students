Homework: Digital Diary

A week with my phone

Duración:
Una semana (escritura corta diaria)
Agrupamiento:
Individual

For next class, you are going to keep a digital diary for ONE WEEK. Each day, write 2–3 sentences in English answering these questions:

  1. How much time did I spend on my phone today?
  2. Which app did I use the most?
  3. Did I doomscroll, feel FOMO, or compare myself with someone? When?
  4. How did I feel?

At the end of the week, write a final paragraph (80–100 words) reflecting on the patterns you noticed.

How to submit

  • Where: Microsoft Teams → channel "The Me I Show Online" → Assignments → "Session 1: Digital diary".
  • Format: Word document (.docx) or PDF.
  • Length: around 200 words in total.
  • Deadline: the day before Session 2 begins, at 23:59.
