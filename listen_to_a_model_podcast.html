Listen to a Model Podcast

A dialogue with my phone

Duración:
15 min
Agrupamiento:
Individual + plenaria

Sample transcript (model)

HUMAN: Phone? Are you awake?
PHONE: I'm always awake. You know that.
HUMAN: Right. Listen, I need to ask you something. Why do you make me feel anxious every time I open Instagram?
PHONE: Wait a minute — I don't make you feel anything. I'm just a screen.
HUMAN: Come on, that's not fair. You vibrate every two minutes!
PHONE: Because YOU told me to. I have ninety-three apps and you allowed all of them to send notifications.
HUMAN: …Oh. So it's my fault?
PHONE: Let's say it's a shared responsibility. How about turning off notifications for at least half of those apps tonight?
HUMAN: Deal. But promise me you'll stop showing me ads for things I mentioned only once.
PHONE: I'll try. No promises.

Analysis questions

  1. How many turns does each speaker have?
  2. Find one example of advice or suggestion. What structure does it use?
  3. Is the conversation formal or informal? How can you tell?
  4. Find a moment of humour. What makes it funny?
  5. How long is this podcast (in seconds)?
