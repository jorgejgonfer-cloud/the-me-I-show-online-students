Vocabulary Focus

Key vocabulary

These are the words you will need throughout the unit. Some of them appeared in the reading, others will come up later.

WordDefinitionExample
to scroll (through)to move text or images up or down on a screen, usually with your finger.I scrolled through Instagram for an hour without realising it.
doomscrollingspending a lot of time reading bad news online.I was doomscrolling instead of going to sleep.
FOMOthe worry that other people are doing more interesting things than you.Looking at her stories gave me serious FOMO.
highlightsthe best or most exciting parts of something.Social media only shows the highlights, not the boring parts.
upward comparisoncomparing yourself with someone you think is better than you.Upward comparison can lower your self-esteem.
self-esteemhow much you value and respect yourself.She has very high self-esteem.
to unfollowto stop receiving updates from someone on social media.I unfollowed accounts that made me feel anxious.
notificationa short message your phone shows you.Turn off notifications to focus better.
screen timethe total time you spend looking at a screen.My screen time was 6 hours yesterday — too much!
digital footprintall the information about you that exists on the Internet.Your digital footprint can affect future job interviews.
