Write Your Script

Your script: step by step

Duración:
40 min
Agrupamiento:
Individual

Now it's your turn. Follow this template to write your own script (around 250–300 words = 2–3 minutes when read aloud).

Structure

  1. OPENING (1 turn each) — Greet your phone and announce the topic.
  2. FIRST QUESTION OR COMPLAINT (2–3 turns) — You bring up something that bothers you. Your phone defends itself.
  3. DEEPER EXCHANGE (2–3 turns) — Use vocabulary from this unit (doomscrolling, FOMO, screen time…).
  4. ADVICE / DEAL (2 turns) — Use "how about…", "why don't you…", "you should…", etc.
  5. CLOSING (1 turn each) — End with humour or a memorable line.

Tips

  • Give your phone a personality. Sarcastic? Polite? Tired? Anxious? Pick one and stick with it.
  • Read your script aloud BEFORE recording. If a line feels strange, change it.
  • Don't write big paragraphs — short, real-sounding sentences work better.
  • Add small interjections: "Hmm…", "Well…", "You know what?"
