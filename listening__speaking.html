Listening & Speaking

Listening: video on digital well-being

Duración:
20 min
Agrupamiento:
Individual + plenaria

As you watch, answer the following questions. You can pause and rewind.

  1. What is the speaker's main argument? Summarise it in one sentence.
  2. Mention TWO concrete examples the speaker gives.
  3. Does the speaker think technology is good, bad, or both? Justify your answer with something they say.
  4. Did anything in the video surprise you? Why?

After watching, share your answers with a partner before discussing them in plenary.

Speaking: My digital diet

Duración:
20 min
Agrupamiento:
Parejas

Work in pairs. You and your partner are going to interview each other about your digital habits. Take notes — at the end you will report back to the class.

Step 1 — Prepare your questions (5 min)

Together with your partner, write 5 questions you want to ask each other. Use these prompts:

  • How long…? / How often…?
  • Have you ever…?
  • What would happen if…?
  • Do you think…?
  • Which apps / accounts…?

Step 2 — Interview your partner (10 min)

Take turns. Listen carefully and ask follow-up questions: "Why?", "Can you give me an example?", "How did you feel?"

Step 3 — Report back (5 min)

Tell the class TWO interesting things you learned about your partner. Use reported speech where appropriate.

Digital support

Use Microsoft Teams to take notes during the interview. You can also record short audio clips using the recording function in Teams or your phone — but ONLY if your partner agrees.
