Useful Phrases

Phrases for a natural dialogue

These phrases will help your podcast sound natural and dynamic. Try to include at least one from each category in your script.

Starting a conversation

  • "Hi, phone. We need to talk."
  • "OK, listen — I have a question for you."
  • "So… here we are. You and me."

Asking questions

  • "Why do you always…?"
  • "How come you…?"
  • "Can you explain why…?"
  • "Don't you think it's a bit weird that…?"

Reacting and showing emotion

  • "Are you serious?"
  • "Wait a minute — that's not fair."
  • "Hmm, I see your point."
  • "OK, fair enough."
  • "That's actually a good idea."

Reaching an agreement

  • "Let's make a deal."
  • "From now on, I'll … if you …"
  • "Deal."
  • "Sounds good to me."

Closing the conversation

  • "Anyway, I should get going."
  • "Talk to you later — but maybe not for two hours."
  • "Goodnight, phone."
