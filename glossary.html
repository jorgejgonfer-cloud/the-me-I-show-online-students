Glossary

Key terms

TermDefinition
digital footprintAll the information about you that exists on the Internet (posts, comments, photos, search history).
doomscrollingSpending a long time scrolling through bad news, even when it makes you feel worse.
FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)The uncomfortable feeling that other people are doing more interesting things than you.
upward social comparisonComparing yourself with someone you think is doing better than you.
highlight reelA selection of only the best moments — the version of life people show on social media.
screen timeThe total time you spend looking at a screen each day.
notificationA short message your phone shows you (usually with a sound or vibration).
mute / unmuteTo turn off / on the sound or alerts of an app or person.
follow / unfollowTo start / stop receiving updates from someone on social media.
block / mute (someone)To stop seeing someone's content (block = they cannot see you either; mute = they don't know).
digital detoxA period of time when you intentionally stop using digital devices.
algorithmThe system that decides what content you see on social media.
echo chamberAn online space where you only see opinions similar to your own.
mindless scrollingScrolling without paying real attention or having a goal.
to be glued to your phoneIdiom: to be unable to stop looking at your phone.
to log off / log outTo leave a website or app, or to disconnect from the Internet.
self-esteemHow much you value and respect yourself.
well-beingThe state of being healthy, happy and comfortable.
phone-freeWithout a phone (e.g. a phone-free dinner).
